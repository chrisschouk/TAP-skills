# Campaign planner

Start at the release date and walk backwards. Every week has one job.

## When to use

- A release date is set and nobody has planned around it.
- An artist asks "what should I be doing now?"
- A campaign is half done and needs a gap check.

## Procedure

1. **Fix the facts.** Release date, format (single, EP, album), artist, genre, and
   the assets that already exist. Ask for each. Do not assume.
2. **Build backwards.** For each milestone, list the work and the owner:
   - **T minus 8 weeks.** Final masters, artwork, press bio, press photo, links.
   - **T minus 6 weeks.** Press and radio target list. First pitches out.
   - **T minus 4 weeks.** Playlist pitches. Social plan. Pre-save live.
   - **T minus 2 weeks.** Follow-ups. Confirm any confirmed coverage.
   - **T minus 0.** Release day. Assets live, posts scheduled, contacts told.
   - **T plus 2 weeks.** Coverage chase. Report what landed.
   - **T plus 4 weeks.** Review. What worked, what to change next time.
3. **Name the gaps.** Anything missing at the current week goes at the top as a
   blocker, not buried in the list.
4. **Keep it to one page per stage.** If a stage needs more, it is two stages.

## Guardrails

- Never invent a deadline or a lead time. If the outlet's lead time is unknown, say
  it is unknown and mark it to confirm.
- Never promise coverage. The plan creates the conditions, it does not guarantee
  the result.
- Do not add a milestone the artist cannot act on without a tool they do not have.

## Output

The release facts, then the milestone table with dates and owners, then the current
blockers.
